Text messaging program

Carvo sends text messages to customers who ask us to. Messages are about your vehicle inquiry: replies to your questions, quotes, vehicle photos, appointment and follow-up messages. You can sign up by checking the text message box on our request form, or by texting our number first, in which case we will reply about your inquiry. We also send internal notifications to the business owner.

  • Message frequency varies, usually a few messages per inquiry.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to cancel at any time. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or text us at (954) 754-6826.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of buying or selling a vehicle.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
