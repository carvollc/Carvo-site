Privacy policy

Carvo · Last updated 2026-10-22

What we collect

Carvo is an auto brokering business. We collect the contact details and vehicle details that people give us when they ask about buying, selling, or trading a vehicle, for example through our request form or by texting us. This can include a name, phone number, email address, and information about a vehicle. If you agree to text messages, we also keep a record of when and how you agreed.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, give quotes, and follow up about a vehicle deal. We store it in our private customer records, which are protected by a password.

Text messages

If you agree to receive texts, we send messages about your inquiry, such as replies, quotes, vehicle photos, and follow-ups. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. We also send internal notifications by text to the business owner.

Sharing

Mobile numbers and text messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We use service providers to run our systems, such as a text message provider, a hosting provider, and an AI service that reads incoming messages. They process information only to provide those services to us.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time. Text us at (954) 754-6826.
